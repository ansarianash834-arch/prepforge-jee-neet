High-Weightage Quick Revision Topics

Exam mein sabse zyada baar puche jaane wale direct formula & NCERT points:

1. Physics — Laws of Motion & Work-Power: • Pulley Tension: T = [2m₁m₂ / (m₁ + m₂)]g & Acceleration a = [(m₁ - m₂) / (m₁ + m₂)]g.
• Maximum hanging chain length without slipping: L_hang = [μ / (μ + 1)] × L.
• Banking of Road safe speed: v = √[rg(tanθ + μ) / (1 - μtanθ)].
2. Physics — Electrostatics & Current: • Identical spheres touched: New charge on each q = (q₁ + q₂) / 2.
• Wire stretched n times: New Resistance R' = n²R.
• Wheatstone Bridge balanced condition: P / Q = R / S (central galvanometer current = 0).
3. Chemistry — Equilibrium, Kinetics & Electrochemistry: • Solubility Product for AₓBᵧ: Ksp = xˣ · yʸ · S^(x+y).
• First Order Reaction: t₁/₂ = 0.693 / k (independent of initial concentration) & t₉₉.₉% = 10 × t₁/₂.
• Nernst Equation at 298 K: E_cell = E°_cell - (0.0591 / n) log Q.
4. Biology — Genetics & Biotech Must-Knows: • Linear DNA cut with 'n' restriction sites gives (n + 1) fragments; Circular plasmid gives 'n' fragments.
• Number of gametes = 2ⁿ (where n = number of heterozygous pairs).
• PCR steps order: Denaturation (94°C) → Annealing (54°C) → Extension (72°C with Taq Polymerase).
